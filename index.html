Sources and data
  • Wind, sea state, swell, currents and sea temperature: Open-Meteo weather and marine APIs (CC BY 4.0), refreshed live.
  • Tides: LINZ predictions for Auckland, Marsden Point and Tauranga, built in (each saved spot uses its nearest port), © LINZ, CC BY 4.0. Secondary port offsets.
  • No-take areas (marine reserves and Hauraki Gulf high protection areas): Department of Conservation, © Crown, CC BY 4.0, built in (), boundaries simplified to about 20 m. Not for navigation or legal interpretation: check DOC’s maps.
  • Moon, sun and bite windows: calculated in your browser from standard solar/lunar formulas.
  • Angler reports: fishing.net.nz saltwater reports (built-in, 17 Sep 2026) plus any AI updates you load.
  • Species share: MPI National Panel Survey 2022–23, FMA1 harvest.
  • Rules: MPI Auckland & Kermadec rules; DOC Hauraki Gulf protection areas; tarakihi limit; hāpuku/bass limit; rock lobster closures; rockpool closure.
  • Big game: fishing.net.nz gamefish guide; NZ Sport Fishing Council; El Niño and NZ fishing; NIWA outlook Sep–Nov 2026.
  • Map: MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3); base map OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles © OpenStreetMap contributors; satellite imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics. Not for navigation.
  • Fish photos: Wikimedia Commons; each photographer and licence is credited under the photo.
  • Catches and their photos are stored only on this device: use Back up on the Catches tab to keep a copy.
  • Your spots, and any offline map you save, are stored only in this browser on this device. Offline map tiles © OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Pressure, ocean current: Open-Meteo (currents from Météo-France SMOC). Distances and bearings are from your phone’s GPS. Not for navigation.
